> When the stars are few and far between, make your own to light the night

It's a Sparrow! It's a Tolly! It's a... modded faction! This project was started in August 2023, back when modding was a far off goal of the ATS devs, and not entirely clear on if it could be done. 2 years later and sudden Laps pulled modding capabilities out of nowhere.

This is still very much a Work In Progress.

This faction's themes: Energy and Modularity
All weapons, in normal sci-fi shenanigans, would be considered energy weapons(laser/plasma/exotic).

Weaponry:
- Plasma Launcher: Explosive bullets
- Laser: Rapid fire PD/Anti-Light beam
- Missile: Extremely fast, low HP singularity missiles with a bit of shred.
- Breach Cannon: Effectively infinite piercing burst beam. Low DPS for its range. (Fryhart beware)
- Plasma Railgun: Kontaalen Plasma damage + Vaalkorai Railgun speed. May still be busted.

Balance is intended to be Vanilla. If it is not, suggestions are welcome. I do not foresee this being entirely balanced correctly at first, given the relatively low sample size Platform Wars and my own testing can provide. Brawler weapons are still not entirely locked down. May rework them some more, we'll see...
Structural turrets not implemented yet due to key support items not implemented yet.

Economy is probably in tatters balance wise... still need to make matter storage(sorry! It's all modeled and everything for ~2 years now, dw about it).

I *do* have ideas for lore, I just haven't written most of it down. Most, as in there's a tiny bit of flavor on some few things.

I'll post a new version soon with patch notes from the last version(includes mini platform since I apparently forgot to make that too). Also getting rid of the temporary project name from the folder.

## Installation
Check for the zip under the release you want to download <a href="https://github.com/Alophox/Anesterian-Research-Conclave/releases">here</a>, and move it into the Local Mods folder(Foreign does not currently work); you might need to unzip it, I haven't tried. The Main menu in ATS has a button on the bottom right that will open your local file explorer directly there. From there, Start (or restart) ATS.
There will be mod warnings about mesh "" being missing; these are expected and can be ignored. Eventually I'll clean them up but for now they are harmless.

## Using the mod
To play with the mod in Skirmish, ensure you check the "Included Modded Factions" on the bottom right of the Skirmish menu. Mods do not currently have access to AI, so ensure to disable any modded factions from being used by AI.
